# Anti-monochrome accent wildcard — HIGH / DARK FANTASY variant.
# Same principle as the cyberpunk set: each line forces at least TWO contrasting accent hues,
# placed on wardrobe / lighting / environment (NOT skin), so a dominant saturated color
# (e.g. tiefling-red skin, red demon) has something to contrast against instead of flooding.
# Uses fantasy-native light/material sources (firelight, moonlight, arcane glow, gemstones,
# gilt, witchfire, stained glass) — no neon or signage. Inject alongside the outfit, or pin
# one line into the base prompt. Strongest paired with the base-prompt color-separation line.

# WARM-vs-COOL LIGHT
warm torchlight against cold blue moonlight, a split-lit scene
firelight amber against a cool arcane-blue glow
candlelight-gold warmth against silver starlight
lantern-orange highlights against deep teal shadow
a hearth-warm figure against a cold moonlit environment

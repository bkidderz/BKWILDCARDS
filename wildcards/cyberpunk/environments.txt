# CYBERPUNK ENVIRONMENTS - KREA2 (natural-language prose)
# Neon-noir high-tech low-life: rain-slick streets under holographic
# advertising, LED signage, chrome and glass, fiber-optic data trunks and
# AR overlays - balanced evenly against Neuromancer/Sprawl grime and
# quasi-biohorror: black clinics, vat-grown flesh, wet concrete, sodium
# haze, rust and rot.
# Sources: Cyberpunk 2077 & Edgerunners, Shadowrun 6th edition, Blade Runner,
# Neuromancer / the Sprawl trilogy, modern cyberpunk installations.
# Distinctly NOT cassette futurism: no molded beige plastic, analog needle
# gauges, toggle switches, or monochrome segment readouts as the keynote.
# Person-free settings. Pair with __cyberpunk_pose__ and an outfit line.
# One environment per line. 18 interiors + 6 exteriors.

# ============================================================
# CASSETTE FUTURISM ACCENT PALETTE - KREA2
# Anti-monochrome accent wildcard - RETRO ANALOG variant.
# Each line forces at least TWO contrasting accent hues placed on wardrobe /
# lighting / panels / readouts (NOT skin), so a dominant color has something
# to contrast against instead of flooding the frame.
# Uses cassette-futurism-native sources ONLY: monochrome CRT phosphor (amber
# and green), incandescent panel bulbs, small indicator lamps, hazard striping,
# molded beige/grey plastic, stenciled institutional paint. NO neon, NO
# holograms, NO signage glow. Inject alongside the outfit, or pin one line in.
# ============================================================

# Warm vs Cool
warm amber CRT phosphor glow against cool green readout light
amber segment-display warmth against a cold cathode-green screen wash
incandescent panel-bulb orange against cool phosphor-green light
a warm amber-lit interior against cold green monitor glow

# Signal
faded safety-orange hazard striping against institutional grey-green panels
warning-red indicator lamps against muted teal control housings
caution-yellow stencil markings against dull slate-blue plastic
a single red standby lamp against a field of cool grey switch panels

# Institutional
faded mustard-yellow and washed-teal accents across the wardrobe and panels
dull brick-orange and slate-blue accents through the scene
muted olive-drab and warm-tan accents across the outfit and background
chalky sky-blue and warm ochre accents on scuffed plastic housings

# Metallic
brushed aluminium and warm-amber lamp accents against a beige plastic field
gunmetal toggle banks and a single orange power light
oxidized brass fittings against cool grey enamel panels
scuffed chrome trim and warm incandescent accents against matte plastic

# Multicolor
a bank of small indicator lamps in separated red, amber and green
mixed labeled buttons in faded red, blue and yellow kept visually distinct
a control board of several muted signal colors held clearly separated

# Subject vs Environment
a warm-toned subject set against a distinctly cool-toned environment
a cool-toned subject set against a distinctly warm-toned environment
a saturated subject against a muted, desaturated background
the wardrobe in hues that deliberately contrast the subject's own coloring

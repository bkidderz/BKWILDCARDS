# HALLOWEEN COSTUMES - KREA2 (natural-language prose)
# Classic (1950-79), cute/glam, and modern/franchise (1980-present) Halloween
# costumes AS WORN BY A PERSON. Every line must read as a subject dressed in a
# costume for Halloween - never the actual creature, monster, or character.
#
# COSTUME, NOT CREATURE / NOT METATYPE: makeup is applied costume face paint
# over natural skin; blood is theatrical fake blood; monsters and franchise
# creatures (zombie, werewolf, TMNT, Pikachu, E.T.) are a costumed human,
# explicitly not the real being.
#
# MASKS & HELMETS: full-coverage helmets/masks state the hair is completely
# hidden and no hair is visible; pushed-up boxed masks state the wearer's hair
# shows beneath; face-only masks state the hair is left visible around them -
# so hair-wildcard lines never blend into the headwear.
#
# One costume per line. Color/option variants pre-expanded to individual lines.
# ============================================================

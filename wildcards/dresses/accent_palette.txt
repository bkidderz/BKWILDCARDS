# GOWNS & DRESSES ACCENT PALETTE - KREA2
# Anti-monochrome accent wildcard - FORMAL / COUTURE variant.
# Each line forces at least TWO contrasting accent hues placed on wardrobe /
# lighting / environment (NOT skin), so a dominant color has something to
# contrast against instead of flooding the frame.
# Uses elegant, luminous sources: silk and velvet, gemstone and pearl, gilt
# and silver, chandelier warmth, moonlight, soft daylight. NO neon, NO signage.
# Inject alongside the gown, or pin one line into the base prompt.

# Jewel
deep-sapphire silk against warm champagne-gold accents
emerald-green velvet against soft blush-rose accents
ruby-red gown against cool silver-and-crystal highlights
amethyst-violet against warm antique-gold accents
deep-garnet against pale ice-blue accents

# Pastel
blush-pink against sage-green accents
powder-blue against warm ivory-and-gold accents
lavender against soft butter-yellow accents

# Metallic
champagne-gold beading against cool marble-white
antique-silver embroidery against deep midnight-blue
rose-gold accents against soft dove-grey
warm-gilt trim against a cool moonlit environment

# Warm vs Cool
warm chandelier-gold against cool moonlight through the windows, a split-lit scene
candle-warm highlights against cool blue evening shadow
warm gilt interior against a cool silver garden beyond
a warm-lit figure against a cool marble setting

# Multicolor
an iridescent gown catching several distinct jewel hues
a rich polychromatic couture palette, colors kept visually distinct
opalescent fabric shifting between separated pastel hues

# Subject vs Environment
a warm-toned subject set against a distinctly cool-toned environment
a cool-toned subject set against a distinctly warm-toned environment
a saturated subject against a muted, desaturated background
the wardrobe in hues that deliberately contrast the subject's own coloring
